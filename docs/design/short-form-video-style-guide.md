# LUXOR9 — Short-Form Video Style Guide

**Status:** Proposed GTM creative standard  
**Format:** Vertical reels (9:16), 20–30 seconds  
**Brand alignment:** Obsidian / mineral ivory / restrained signal cyan; doctrine *Control AI execution. Prove every outcome.*  
**Scope:** Luxor9 only. No third-party firm names (including AG Associates). No invented customers, results, or metrics.

---

## 1. Purpose

This guide locks how Luxor9 appears in short-form video: one lifecycle language, one edit grammar, one sound grammar, one closer. Creators use it for hooks, cut lists, captions, and end cards so every reel feels institutional—not promotional gimmickry.

**Primary wedge illustrated here:** CA GST exception desk (proposed). The same beat map and closer apply to other verticals without changing the signature.

---

## 2. Non-negotiables

| Rule | Spec |
|------|------|
| Brand only | On-screen and spoken brand is **LUXOR9** (or Luxor9). No partner co-brands, no agency names, no “powered by” third parties. |
| Evidence honesty | Never invent customers, case studies, ROI, time-saved claims, or volume metrics. Label illustrative figures as **example**. Cite when a number is sourced. |
| Sourced benchmark | ICAI estimates **3–8 hours per client per month** for GSTR-2B reconciliation. Use that range only with clear attribution; do not extrapolate firm-wide savings. |
| Tone | Premium, institutional, confident. Calm authority. Short sentences. Prefer control and proof verbs over hype. |
| Visual system | Full-bleed or edge-to-edge frames on **obsidian** (`#0B0D10`) with **mineral ivory** (`#F3EFE6`) type and **signal cyan** (`#2EC4B6`) as a restrained accent—never purple stacks, emoji, sticker badges, or multi-layer glow. |
| Motion budget | One primary effect per lifecycle beat. No effect stacks in a single shot. Prefer decisive cuts over continuous noise. |
| UI honesty | Product UI may be stylized; never fake live production evidence IDs or approval outcomes as customer proof. Demo frames may use synthetic IDs in the form `LX9/EVT/######` when needed for clarity. |

## 3. Lifecycle beat map (edit + sound)

Method line (on-screen microcopy, optional): `KNOW → THINK → ACT → PROVE → LEARN`

| Beat | Meaning in frame | Editing effect | Sound cue |
|------|------------------|----------------|-----------|
| **KNOW** | Exception or signal enters attention | **Flash cut** — hard cut with a single-frame ivory flash (≤2 frames) into the new image | Dry **data-tick** (short high click, no reverb) |
| **THINK** | Pattern links problem to structured view | **Match cut** — shape, motion, or layout continuity across the cut (e.g. mismatch row → exception queue row) | Soft **harmonic step** (one ascending interval, muted) |
| **ACT** | Human authorizes; system proceeds under policy | **B&W → color** — desaturated frame resolves to full color on the approval / commit moment | Solid **low confirm** (short muted thud; no whoosh) |
| **PROVE** | Evidence and provenance become visible | **X-ray** — brief translucency / layer peel revealing source, timestamp, claim link, verification state | Thin **glass slide** (clean scrape; stop cleanly) |
| **LEARN** | Outcome returns to the operating loop | **Light leak / glow** — restrained mineral-ivory or signal-cyan edge bloom (one pass, ≤0.4s); never neon flare | Quiet **sustain lift** (half-second pad; fade under closer) |

### Effect discipline

- Assign at most one of the five effects to any shot that carries a named beat.
- Do not use flash cut for ACT, or X-ray for KNOW—the map is fixed so the audience learns the grammar.
- Reduced-motion / accessibility cutdowns: replace flash cut with a hard cut + caption; replace light leak with a static ivory hairline; keep match cut / B&W-to-color / X-ray as stills with captions if motion must be removed.

## 4. Signature closer

Every reel ends on the same locked end card (last 2.0–2.5 seconds).

**Frame**

- Full-bleed **obsidian**.
- Centered wordmark: **LUXOR** in mineral ivory + **9** in signal cyan (match Control Tower brand treatment).
- One doctrine line beneath, ivory at reduced weight: `Control AI execution. Prove every outcome.`
- Optional micro-line (muted): `KNOW · THINK · ACT · PROVE · LEARN`
- No logos of other firms, no QR codes unless a campaign explicitly authorizes one, no sticker CTAs on the wordmark.

**Sound**

- Signature closer sound: **two-note identity cadence** — soft low tone then a slightly higher restrained cyan-associated chime (≈120–180 ms apart), then silence under the hold.
- Do not layer voiceover under the closer; VO ends before the cadence starts.
- Same cadence on every reel; do not vary for “hooks.”

**On-screen CTA (optional, one line only)**

- Prefer: `Governed exception desks — Luxor9`
- Avoid: urgency countdowns, emoji, “Link in bio” as hero type.

## 5. Reel structure template

| Segment | Duration | Requirement |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| Hook | 0.0–2.0s | One problem sentence or visual mismatch; brand may appear as small corner wordmark after 1s, not as the hook itself |
| Body | ~2–25s | Shot list; each shot tagged with lifecycle beat + effect when applicable |
| Closer | final 2.0–2.5s | Signature frame + sound only |

**Voiceover:** measured, mid-register, no parody or meme cadence.  
**On-screen text:** short lines; prefer verbs of control and proof.  
**Music bed:** optional sparse drone under body; duck under VO; never compete with beat cues or the closer cadence.

## 6. Wedge context — CA GST exception desk (proposed)

**Problem shape (honest):** Monthly GSTR-2B reconciliation is labour-intensive for CA practices. ICAI estimates **3–8 hours per client per month** for GSTR-2B reconciliation (sourced). Luxor9 is positioned as a governed **exception desk**: surface mismatches, structure review, require human authority for consequential actions, attach evidence, and feed outcomes back into the loop.

**Do not claim:** automatic filing, guaranteed ITC recovery, named firm results, or hours saved beyond the sourced ICAI range used as context—not as Luxor9 performance.

- Visual: Dense spreadsheet / portal mismatch rows, slightly crushed contrast on obsidian crop.  
- On-screen text: `GSTR-2B still eats the month.`

**Shot list**

## 8. Copy bank (safe lines)

**Allowed**

- “Governed exception desk for GST review.”
- “Human authority before consequential action.”
- “Evidence with source, time, and verification status.”
- “ICAI estimates 3–8 hours per client per month for GSTR-2B reconciliation.” *(sourced; do not turn into Luxor9 savings)*
- “Queue: twelve open mismatches (example).”

**Forbidden**

- Named customers or “leading CA firm in …” without a real, approved case.
- “Save 3–8 hours with Luxor9” (misuses the ICAI estimate as product performance).
- AG Associates or any non-Luxor9 firm branding.
- Guaranteed ITC, filing automation claims, or invented accuracy percentages.
- Meme audio, parody CA stereotypes, emoji end cards.
