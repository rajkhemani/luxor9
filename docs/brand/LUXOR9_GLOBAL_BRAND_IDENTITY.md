## 3. Logo Engineering (`X`-Unit System)

- **Symbol:** *The Governed Keystone* — 9X square outer matrix (base unit `1X` = primary stroke thickness = 1/9 symbol height). Combines an architectural `L` foundation and calibrated `9` chamber bisected by the **Signal Meridian**.
- **Clear Space:** Minimum `2X` perimeter on all four sides.
- **Symbol-to-Wordmark Gap:** `3.5X` horizontal spacing; wordmark cap-height `5.2X`, optical tracking `+180`.
- **Minimum Sizes:** Digital symbol `24px` / full lockup `120px`; Print symbol `8mm` / full lockup `28mm`.
- **Small-Scale UI Variant:** Optically thickened stroke (`+14%`) for `16px–22px` interface slots.

## 6. Motion, Acoustic & Multi-Agent Voice Identity

- **Durations:** Fast `160ms` · Standard `240ms` · Deliberate Governance `380ms` · Curve `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
- **Zero-Theatrics Rule:** Never use comical error shakes, confetti, or pulsing neon blooms. Always honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **6 Unified Specialist Voice Profiles:**
  - `EX-01` Executive Intelligence (138–148 WPM · Calm, boardroom-concise)
  - `MK-02` Market Intelligence (140–152 WPM · Analytical, evidence-led)
  - `LD-03` Lead Intelligence (150–162 WPM · Crisp, commercially aware)
  - `SO-04` Sales Operations (145–155 WPM · Confident, consultative)
  - `CO-05` Customer Operations (136–146 WPM · Warm, composed)
  - `DC-06` Document Intelligence (134–144 WPM · Precise, neutral, methodical)
- **Zero-Hallucination Spoken Standard:** If a price, handover date, or offer lacks a verified source, the agent pauses execution and states `"Source needed"`—never guessing.
